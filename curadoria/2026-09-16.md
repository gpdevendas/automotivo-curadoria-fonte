## Novidades e lançamentos — Internacional

**McLaren to invest $675 million to expand UK manufacturing, launch ...** — Reuters, 16/09.
Fato: McLaren is to invest £500 million ($675 million) in UK manufacturing and engineering, aiming to launch its first SUV model and create 1,000 jobs by 2032. The investment includes a new assembly plant and in-house development of transmissions and engines. McLaren seeks to enhance profit margins, targeting the high-end luxury market to compete with brands like Ferrari. While shifting focus on expanding its product range, CEO Nick Collins noted no immediate plans for electric vehicles due to lack of customer demand. The move comes amid industry restructuring, with McLaren hoping to improve financial performance after years of losses.
