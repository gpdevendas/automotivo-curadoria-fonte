## Novidades e lançamentos — Internacional

**Volvo Cars launches longer-range versions of its hybrid XC60 and ...** — Reuters, 15/09.
Fato: Volvo Cars has launched new hybrid variants of its XC60 and XC90 models, featuring significantly longer electric ranges for both the European and American markets. The electric range for the XC60 is now up to 200 kilometers per charge, while the XC90 can achieve up to 160 kilometers due to a larger battery and a more powerful electric motor. These long-range hybrids are positioned as a transitional step toward Volvo's goal of becoming fully electric in the future. Both models will also continue to be offered as mild hybrids.
