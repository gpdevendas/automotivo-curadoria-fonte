**Scarlett Johansson substitui Jason Bateman como voz da Hyundai** — Meio e Mensagem - Marketing, Mídia e Comunicação, 09/09.
Fato: Scarlett Johansson replaces Jason Bateman as the voice of Hyundai in a new campaign for the charity "Hope on Wheels," which supports children's cancer initiatives. The campaign, created by Innocean, features a girl triumphing over cancer in a gladiator arena. It will debut during the NFL opening on September 9. Johansson's collaboration with Hyundai aligns with the brand's commitment to social responsibility, and she is expected to continue narrating future ads. Hyundai emphasizes its long-term investment in the U.S. market, having been established there for 40 years, while planning to support the campaign across various media throughout September, National Childhood Cancer Awareness Month.
