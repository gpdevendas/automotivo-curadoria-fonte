## Indústria automotiva, produção e investimentos

**Mais da metade dos carros importados pelo Brasil já vem da China** — G1, 08/09.
Fato: A recent report from Anfavea highlights that over half of imported cars in Brazil now come from China, with the country producing 271,200 vehicles in August, the highest monthly output since October 2019. The growth in production is largely attributed to assembly methods such as CKD (Completely Knocked Down) and SKD (Semi Knocked Down), which account for two-thirds of the increase this year.
Leitura: O impacto prático está em disponibilidade de produto, prazo, capacidade industrial e pressão competitiva sobre a rede e os fornecedores.
[Fonte](https://g1.globo.com/carros/noticia/2026/09/08/resultado-producao-anfavea.ghtml)

**General Motors quer importar veículos dos EUA, mas há entraves** — Automotive Business, 10/09.
Fato: General Motors (GM) aims to increase its vehicle offerings in Brazil by importing cars from the U.S., targeting an estimated 3,000 vehicles annually if regulatory conditions improve. However, regulatory differences in vehicle safety and emissions between the U.S. and Brazil pose significant challenges. Fabio Rua, GM's VP for South America, highlighted efforts to negotiate with the Brazilian government to allow U.S. standards, already accepted in neighboring countries like Chile and Uruguay. Aside from regulatory hurdles, the financial burden of import taxes complicates the situation, as Brazil lacks a free trade agreement with the U.S. that would permit zero-tariff imports. ## Marketing automotivo

**Band lança campanha "Desate o Nó" sobre saúde mental** — Meio e Mensagem - Marketing, Mídia e Comunicação, 11/09.
Fato: The Band Group has launched the 'Desate o Nó' campaign aimed at raising awareness for mental health and suicide prevention during September Yellow. The initiative, developed with BETC Havas and the Instituto Bandeirantes, kicked off on September 10, coinciding with World Suicide Prevention Day, through a live intervention on Jornal da Band where presenters used loosened ties as a symbol for addressing emotional knots. The campaign will be promoted across various media platforms, offering informative ads about psychological support services, including the CVV's hotline (188). The overarching message encourages openness about emotions and seeking help.
Leitura: Para marketing, vale observar proposta, canal e prova concreta da campanha, evitando copiar formato sem validar aderência ao público e à operação comercial.
