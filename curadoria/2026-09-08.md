## Novidades e lançamentos — Internacional

**UK car registrations rise for ninth straight month - Reuters** — Reuters, 04/09.
Fato: In August, UK car sales increased by 13.7% year-on-year, marking the ninth month of consecutive growth, with total sales reaching 94,236 vehicles. This growth is primarily driven by a 27.7% rise in battery electric vehicle (BEV) registrations, accounting for about 30% of total car sales. Analysts suggest that September will be a crucial month for further sales as consumers often wait for newly registered vehicles released biannually in March and September. The UK government is also exploring options for easing the transition to zero-emission vehicle sales, indicating a review of existing targets that phase out petrol and diesel cars.
Leitura: É um sinal de tendência e concorrência; qualquer efeito para o Brasil depende de confirmação de mercado, homologação, preço e calendário local.
