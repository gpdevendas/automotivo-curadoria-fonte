## Indústria automotiva, produção e investimentos

**Produção de comerciais leves da VW Caminhões e Ônibus poderá ...** — AutoData, 03/09.
Fato: Volkswagen Caminhões e Ônibus is exploring the production of light commercial vehicles, specifically diesel cargo vans, potentially outside of Resende. The company is in discussions with partners in Argentina and Uruguay to localize production, aiming to avoid a 35% import tax. CEO Roberto Cortes and VP Ricardo Alouche noted that partnerships, particularly with SAIC Motor, were essential to expedite the project while keeping costs competitive. If developed independently, the launch timeline and vehicle costs could significantly increase. VW aims for a 20% market share in a segment expected to total 50,000 units annually by 2029.
Leitura: O impacto prático está em disponibilidade de produto, prazo, capacidade industrial e pressão competitiva sobre a rede e os fornecedores.
