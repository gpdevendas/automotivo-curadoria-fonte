## Novidades e lançamentos — Internacional

**Tesla to unveil long-delayed Roadster on Oct. 1, Musk says | Reuters** — Reuters, 12/09.
Fato: Tesla CEO Elon Musk announced that the electric vehicle maker will unveil its long-delayed Roadster on October 1, nearly a decade after its initial preview. A post on social media confirmed the launch date, and it is anticipated that the event may include a demonstration of a special edition featuring SpaceX-developed cold-gas thrusters. Initially unveiled in 2017 with a promise of deliveries starting in 2020, the Roadster program has faced multiple delays. The project has shifted from a high-performance EV concept to a carbon-fiber hypercar after changing its construction plans.
